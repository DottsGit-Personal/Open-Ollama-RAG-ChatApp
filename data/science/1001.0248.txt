Abstract
In this paper, Riemann’s Zeta function with odd positive integer argu ment
is represented as an inﬁnite summation of integer powers of πwith rational
coeﬃcients. Speciﬁc values for Apery’s Constant and Catalan’s Con stant are
then derived.
